Sound familiar?

Three problems, one root worth checking.

Snoring, jaw pain, and daytime exhaustion often trace back to how you breathe and hold your jaw while you sleep. That is exactly what this clinic looks at.

You snore, or you stop breathing

Loud snoring and pauses in breathing can be signs of obstructive sleep apnea. Custom oral appliances are an alternative for people who cannot tolerate CPAP.

Your jaw clicks, locks, or aches

TMJ disorder and orofacial pain can show up as headaches, migraines, and a jaw that tires from chewing or talking. Both are treated here.

You never feel rested

Fatigue that coffee does not fix, even after a full night, is worth a sleep evaluation. Poor sleep also ties into weight and blood sugar.
