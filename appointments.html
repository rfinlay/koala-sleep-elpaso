Your first visit

What to expect, and what to bring.

Preparation varies by the type of visit, but this is the general shape of a first appointment at a Koala® center.

Bring with you

  • Your medical history

    Including any sleep-related documents from a referring physician. The office can also request those directly from the physician's office.

  • A list of current medications
  • Any sleep or pain symptoms you have been tracking

    Snoring, pauses in breathing, jaw pain, headaches, how rested you feel in the morning.

  • Your medical insurance information

    So the office can verify coverage and bill claims on your behalf. Sleep apnea, snoring, and TMJ care are billed to medical insurance, not dental plans.

What happens at the visit

  1. Initial exam and consultation

    The doctor reviews your symptoms and history. If a sleep study is needed, the office provides specific instructions beforehand. A home sleep test is one of the diagnostic options Koala offers.

  2. Treatment recommendations

    Following the exam, you receive treatment recommendations along with a written treatment plan that lists fees and any deposits due at the time of service, before any treatment begins.

  3. Insurance handled by the office

    The office requests prior authorization as needed, submits claims to your medical carrier on your behalf, and works with you to make the best use of your coverage. Payment plans are available.

Insurance and payment

Koala bills medical insurance plans and works with many carriers, including unions and managed care plans. Each plan is different, and the office goes over your specific coverage after the initial exam.

If you are not insured, Koala offers self-pay discounts and payment plans, including no-interest and low-interest plans upon approval.

Accepted: CareCredit, Cherry financing, and in-house financing.
